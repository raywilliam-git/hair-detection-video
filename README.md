# Hair Touch Reminder

A simple web application that gently reminds you when your hand stays in your hair for several seconds.

The goal is to help increase awareness of repetitive hair-touching habits.

## How to use

1. Open the application.
2. Allow access to your webcam.
3. Click **Start Camera**.
4. Keep the page open while you work or study.
5. If your hand stays in your hair for a few seconds, you'll hear a short reminder.

## Your privacy

Your privacy is important.

- No video is recorded.
- Nothing is uploaded.
- Everything runs directly on your device.

## Supported devices

The application works in most modern browsers on:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux

A webcam is required.
